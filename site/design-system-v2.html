Vyn AI · provisional

AI outputs

Every Vyn AI output carries one marker: the neon brand green, a "Vyn AI" label and the Vynnie mark. Outputs show their evidence, and a person makes the final call. Try the states and the actions.

State
Vyn AI summaryFrom 3 min 12 s of video
  • On light grounds, neon is only a fill behind text-primary (9:1). Never use it as text or as a thin border on white, where it's 1.7:1.
  • On background-dark, neon text and marks are fine (9:1), as in the header above.
  • AI copy is warm and factual: "Suggested label", "Check this frame". It never says "Vyn AI has determined".
